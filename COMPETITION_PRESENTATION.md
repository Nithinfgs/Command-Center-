# Mathlet '26 - Three-Minute Presentation

Theme: **Mathematics in Space Exploration**

## Before the judges arrive

1. Open Mission Control on a laptop with a 1280 px or wider display.
2. Select the Saturn V preset and confirm that the Systems Check is visible.
3. Open **3-Min Tour**, reset the timer to 3:00, and keep the first step selected.
4. Keep a local copy available so the demonstration does not depend on internet access.

## Presentation script

### 0:00-0:30 - The question

"Our question is: can mathematics design a safer path to space? Mission Control turns physical laws that we cannot see into an interactive model. We choose inputs, apply equations, observe evidence, and improve the design."

Show the first tour step and state that the same rocket is carried across design, atmospheric testing, flight, and orbit.

### 0:30-1:20 - Build and predict

Open **Build**.

"The Tsiolkovsky rocket equation connects specific impulse and the wet-to-dry mass ratio to Delta-v. The model recalculates Delta-v, thrust-to-weight ratio, burn time, and staging whenever a component changes. Center of mass and center of pressure also reveal whether the rocket may flip."

Add one component, point to the changing values, then use Undo to restore the model.

### 1:20-2:10 - Test and validate

Open **Aero** and choose **Transonic Max-Q**.

"Dynamic pressure is q equals one-half rho v squared. Because speed is squared, aerodynamic stress can rise very quickly. We change Mach number, altitude, vehicle pitch, and wind direction, then compare streamlines, pressure, temperature, and shockwaves. The calculated evidence can be exported for analysis."

Switch once between Streamlines and Pressure Field.

### 2:10-2:45 - Extend the model

Open **Orbit**.

"Newton's inverse-square law connects the next parts of the suite: N-body gravity, orbital manoeuvres, Lagrange points, satellite constellations, and rendezvous. The model shows how the same mathematical idea can explain many different space missions."

Pause and resume the orbital simulation once.

### 2:45-3:00 - Conclusion

Open **Impact**.

"Planetary defense gives our clearest conclusion. Impact energy follows one-half m v squared, while a small early Delta-v grows into a large miss distance over time. Mission Control is creative because it connects the mission, innovative because every result is interactive, and relevant because every screen answers a mathematical space-exploration question."

## Suggested roles for a three-person team

- Presenter 1: problem, modelling cycle, and rocket equation.
- Presenter 2: wind-tunnel validation and live controls.
- Presenter 3: orbital mechanics, planetary defense, and conclusion.

## Fast recovery plan

- If a 3D view is slow, use the 2D Blast Map or return to Build.
- If a control is changed accidentally, reload the relevant preset.
- If time is below 20 seconds, jump directly to the Conclusion step in the tour.
